Strategy
  • Level 1: use E on him to drag the wave and stack passive safely
  • After level 11 + first item look for extended trades and solo kills
  • Push first 2 waves (get level 2 first), crash 2nd wave, ward raptors
  • Let him shove every wave till level 6
  • If the wave is slow-pushing toward you, stay inside the wave to force his Q or AAs to destroy it
  • If he has Q2 (tornado) ready, don't step up — especially early
  • Use R half a second before being knocked up
  • Change your Q-dodge patterns each time
  • He can dash on minions to catch you — watch where your next wave is when fleeing
What NOT To Do
  • Don't poke without passive stacked (his slow AA animations let him stat-check you)
  • Don't prioritize hitting him over farming (he heals back with D-Shield, 2nd Wind, Absorb Life, Fleet)
  • Don't forget to check brushes with a blue ward
  • Don't step up if he has Q2 ready, especially early game
